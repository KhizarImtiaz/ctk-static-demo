How To Use This Script

This is a shooting script — everything you need to record a clean training video without guessing. Each scene below gives you four things:

🖥️ ON SCREEN — what the viewer sees 🖱️ DO — what you click 🎙️ SAY — read this aloud, word for word 💬 CALLOUT — text to add in editing (optional)

Read the SAY lines in a calm, friendly pace. Don't rush. If you fumble a line, pause for 2 seconds and say it again — you'll trim the bad take later. Silence is easy to cut; talking over a mistake is not.

Before You Record — Setup Checklist
  • Pick your tool. Loom (easiest), Camtasia (most polish), or OBS Studio (free). Loom is recommended for a first video.
  • Set recording to 1080p and record the browser window only — not the whole desktop.
  • Use a clean demo login — a training account, not a real customer's live data. No real claimant names or PII on screen.
  • Pre-load the demo claim data (see the scenario box below) so you're not typing long text on camera. Have it ready to paste.
  • Close email, chat, and notifications. A Teams popup mid-recording means re-shooting the scene.
  • Zoom the browser to 110–125% so buttons and text are readable on smaller screens.
  • Use a quiet room and a headset mic if you have one. Phone earbuds beat a laptop mic.
  • Do one practice run of the full click-path with no narration, so the app state is fresh and you know where everything is.
The Demo Claim — Use This Throughout

🚗 Two-Vehicle Intersection Collision

Vehicle 1 — Insured
Driver: John Smith
Travel: Northbound on Main St
Right of way: Had green light
Speed: ~30 mph (posted limit)
Vehicle 2 — Claimant
Driver: Jane Doe
Travel: Eastbound on Oak Ave
Right of way: Ran the stop sign
Speed: Estimated above limit
⚖️ Why this scenario: It produces a clear liability picture — Driver 2 failed to yield and broke the right-of-way and lookout duties. That makes every part of the product (duty breach analysis, assessment, settlement) easy to demonstrate convincingly.

The Full Script
1

Welcome & What You'll Learn

0:20
🖥️ On Screen
Title card or the app login screen. Show the Auto Liability logo / product name. Keep it clean and static.
🎙️ Say
"Welcome to Auto Liability by Claim Toolkit. In the next few minutes, I'll walk you through handling a claim from start to finish — creating it, adding the parties, capturing statements, running the liability assessment, and reaching a settlement. By the end, you'll be ready to work your first claim with confidence. Let's get started."
💬 Callout
On-screen title text: "Auto Liability — Handling a Claim End to End"
2

The Dashboard & Navigation

0:35
🖥️ On Screen
The main dashboard / claims list after login. The list of existing claims and the left or top navigation menu.
🖱️ Do
  • Slowly move the cursor across the main navigation so each section is visible.
  • Hover (don't click) over the claims list to show how claims are organized.
🎙️ Say
"This is your dashboard — the home base for all your claims. Every claim you're working on lives here, and you can see its status at a glance. Across the navigation you'll find the key stages of a claim: the claim details, the parties involved, the investigation, the assessment, and finally negotiation and settlement. We'll touch each of these. First, let's create a brand-new claim."
💬 Callout
Briefly highlight or circle the "New Claim" button to lead into the next scene.
3

Creating a New Claim

0:45
🖱️ Do
  • Click New Claim.
  • Fill the core fields: claim number (or let it auto-generate), date of loss, claim type.
  • Paste prepared values — don't type long entries live.
  • Click Save.
🎙️ Say
"To start a new claim, I'll click 'New Claim.' I'll enter the basics — the date the accident happened and the type of claim. Notice the system can generate the claim number for me, so there's nothing to memorize. Once I've filled in the essentials, I click Save — and just like that, the claim is created and ready to build out."
💬 Callout
When Save succeeds, add a small green checkmark callout: "Claim created ✓"
4

Location, Details & Accident Type

1:00
🖥️ On Screen
The Location, Details & Types page. Map / location field, date & time, accident type dropdown, and the condition fields (weather, lighting, area, speed limit).
🖱️ Do
  • Enter the accident location (Main St & Oak Ave intersection) and date/time.
  • Open the Accident Type dropdown — show the options, select the matching type.
  • Click Save.
🎙️ Say
"Next we capture where and when the accident happened. I'll set the location — the intersection of Main Street and Oak Avenue — and the date and time. Then I'll choose the accident type from this dropdown. Now here's where the product really helps you: once I save these details…"
🤖 Ask CTK Moment
This is the AI highlight. After Save, the conditions fields (Weather, Lighting, Area, Speed Limit) populate automatically a moment later. Pause and let the viewer see it happen. If the fields show an "AI" badge or a subtle update, zoom in on it.
🎙️ Say
"…watch the condition fields. The system's built-in AI, Ask CTK, fills in details like the weather, lighting, and speed limit for that location and time — automatically, in the background. You didn't have to look any of that up. It just appears, and you're free to adjust it if needed."
💬 Callout
Add a green callout near the auto-filled fields: "Filled automatically by Ask CTK AI"
5

Adding Parties & Vehicles

0:50
🖥️ On Screen
The Parties section. Form to add a driver, their vehicle, and role in the accident.
🖱️ Do
  • Add Vehicle 1 — John Smith: driver name, vehicle, direction of travel (Northbound, Main St).
  • Save, then add Vehicle 2 — Jane Doe: Eastbound, Oak Ave.
  • Show the two parties now listed together.
🎙️ Say
"Now let's add the people involved. Our first driver is John Smith, traveling north on Main Street. I'll enter his details and his vehicle. Then I'll add the second driver, Jane Doe, who was heading east on Oak Avenue. You can add as many parties as the accident involves — drivers, passengers, and witnesses. With both drivers entered, we have the full picture of who was on the scene."
💬 Callout
Optional: label each party card on screen — "Insured" and "Claimant" — to keep viewers oriented.
6

Facts & Statements + Ask CTK Summary

1:00
🖥️ On Screen
The Facts / Statements editor for a party. A text area for the statement plus the duty-related fields.
🖱️ Do
  • Select John Smith. Paste his statement (he had the green light, was traveling at the limit, the other car ran the stop sign).
  • Click Save.
🎙️ Say
"Here's where we record what each party says happened. I'll open John Smith's statement and enter his account — he had the green light, was traveling at the speed limit, when the other vehicle ran the stop sign and entered the intersection. I'll save that. Now, watch what happens next."
🤖 Ask CTK Moment
Key AI demo. After saving, a few seconds later an AI Summary appears for the statement (with a badge and a timestamp like "Generated 10 seconds ago"). Pause and let it appear on camera. If there's a Regenerate button, point to it.
🎙️ Say
"Within a few seconds, Ask CTK reads the statement and writes a clean, structured summary for you — the direction of travel, the speed, who had the right of way, and any contradictions. You saved the statement; the AI did the analysis. And if you ever want a fresh take, just click Regenerate. This works the same way for every party's statement."
💬 Callout
Green callout over the summary: "AI-generated summary — no waiting, no extra clicks"
7

Investigation Checklist

0:45
🖥️ On Screen
The Investigation Checklist page — checklist items and the Legal Summary block.
🖱️ Do
  • Check off a few investigation items (police report obtained, photos collected, witness contacted).
  • Click Save. Show the Legal Summary populating.
🎙️ Say
"The investigation checklist keeps you organized and makes sure nothing gets missed. I'll mark the steps we've completed — the police report is in, photos are collected, and we've spoken with a witness. As I save these, the system pulls together a legal summary, giving you a running picture of where the investigation stands and what still needs attention."
🤖 Ask CTK Moment
If the Legal Summary is AI-generated in your build, mention it: "and again, Ask CTK helps draft this summary for you." If not, skip that line.
8

Assessment & Duty Breaches

1:30
⭐

This is the most important scene

The Assessment is the heart of the product. Slow down here. This is where the liability decision gets made, and where the AI's value is clearest.

🖥️ On Screen
The Assessment page — Suggested Assessment, Point of Impact, and the Actual Assessment with the four duties: Right of Way, Speed, Lookout, and Avoidance.
🖱️ Do
  • Show the Suggested Assessment and Point of Impact the AI has prepared.
  • Walk through each duty for both drivers: Right of Way, Speed, Lookout, Avoidance.
  • Mark Jane Doe as having breached Right of Way and Lookout (she ran the stop sign).
  • Save and show the Overall Duty Breach Summary.
🎙️ Say
"Now we reach the assessment — this is where we determine who's at fault and why. The system gives you a suggested assessment and even identifies the likely point of impact to get you started. Liability comes down to four driving duties: right of way, speed, lookout, and avoidance. For each driver, we evaluate whether they met or breached each one."
🎙️ Say
"In our case, John Smith had the green light and was driving at the limit — he met his duties. Jane Doe, however, ran the stop sign, so she breached her right-of-way duty and her duty to keep a proper lookout. I'll record those breaches here."
🤖 Ask CTK Moment
After saving, the Overall Duty Breach Summary and the per-duty party summaries fill in via AI. Let them appear. Scroll gently so each AI-written summary is visible.
🎙️ Say
"As I record the breaches, Ask CTK writes up the duty breach summaries for each party and an overall summary of the evidence. This is the analysis that normally takes the longest to write by hand — and here it's done for you, grounded in the statements and facts you've already entered. You review it, adjust if needed, and move on."
💬 Callout
Callout over the four duties: "4 duties: Right of Way · Speed · Lookout · Avoidance". Then green callout on the summaries: "AI-written duty breach analysis"

🖥️ On Screen
The Negotiation & Settlement page — evidence of duty breaches and the settlement entry.

🎙️ Say
"Finally, we move to negotiation and settlement. Notice that the evidence of duty breaches we built in the assessment flows straight through to here — both drivers' right-of-way summaries are ready to support your position. With the liability picture clear, I'll record the settlement terms and save. The claim is now fully documented, from the first detail to the final settlement — with a complete trail behind every decision."

Callout: "Evidence flows automatically from Assessment → Settlement"

After You Record — Polish & Publish
  • Trim the dead air at the start and end of each scene, and cut any fumbled lines.
  • Add the text callouts from the 💬 lines — especially the green "Ask CTK" labels. They make the AI value obvious even with sound off.
  • Add a simple intro/outro card with the product name. Loom and Camtasia both have built-in templates.
  • Turn on auto-captions — many viewers watch training with sound off, and it improves accessibility.
  • Keep zoom-ins for the AI moments (Scenes 4, 6, 8). A gentle zoom draws the eye to the auto-filled fields.
  • Export at 1080p MP4 and upload to the app's Training section.
💡

Pro tip: record it in modules

If 8 minutes feels long, record each scene as its own short clip and publish them as a numbered series ("1. Creating a Claim," "2. Adding Parties," and so on). Shorter videos are easier to re-record when the product changes, and learners can jump to exactly the part they need.

🔒

Before you publish — one last check

Scrub the final video for any real claimant names, policy numbers, addresses, or other PII that may have slipped in. Use the demo scenario data only.
